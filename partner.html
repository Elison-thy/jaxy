When you get to know our partners you will understand why we love what we do! Elephante exists to support leaders in Northern Uganda. Each partner brings a unique flavor of innovation, courage, and hope.

HAVEN for humanity

Haven for Humanity Uganda is a community-rooted nonprofit in Northern Uganda and the West Nile region addressing child malnutrition and family vulnerability with long-term, dignity-based solutions. Through integrated pillars in health, nutrition, economic empowerment, psychosocial support, and education, HHU provides treatment and prevention services while strengthening families through savings groups and income-generating activities. Centered on women's leadership and community resilience, HHU walks alongside families to build stability and opportunity - not short-term aid.

Gender Tech Initiative

Gender Tech Initiative Uganda equips women and girls in STEM and ICT with the skills, mentorship, and support they need to build technology careers. The initiative addresses barriers like online harassment, limited early exposure, and lack of mentorship through programs in digital safety, cybersecurity, digital marketing, and school ICT clubs. Gender Tech helps participants use technology to grow businesses, stay safe online, and step into leadership.

Wokober Education

Wokober Education Foundation is reimagining education in Northern Uganda by shifting learning from exams to creativity, experimentation, and problem-solving. Built on the belief that children and youth have immense potential that systems often limit, Wokober uses hands-on STEAM learning, innovation clubs, and community programs to help learners build, design, and create solutions rooted in Acholi lived experience.

River Poultry

River Poultry is transforming poultry farming in Northern Uganda with technology that strengthens farmer livelihoods and improves food security. Using AI-powered tools, the company helps farmers monitor flock health, access vaccinations, and manage farms efficiently, reducing mortality and lowering costs.

VISIT WEBSITE →

AJURA Styles

Ajura Styles is rewriting fashion in Northern Uganda by proving that African clothing can be ethical, globally relevant, and deeply personal. In response to fast fashion and second-hand imports, Ajura creates locally produced garments using biodegradable linen, natural dyes, and eco-printing. The brand trains and employs young women in tailoring, dyeing, and printing, building real jobs and lasting skills.

AFRO Lab

Afro Lab is changing the rhythm of Gulu by creating a safe, inclusive space where young people can learn, create, and build futures through music and art. Blending traditional African storytelling with modern sounds, Afro Lab supports healing, self-expression, and skill-building for vulnerable youth. Their work has mentored young DJs and artists, built youth-led creative teams, and organized major community events.

Christ Central Church

Christ Central Community Church is a Gospel-centered church in Gulu, founded in 2019 to preach Christ with clarity and see lives transformed. The church reaches villages facing poverty and spiritual neglect through monthly outreach and Bible distribution, while also strengthening leaders through training and a radio ministry.

Stress Clinic Uganda

Stress Clinic Uganda is a mental health and community development organization serving marginalized communities in Northern Uganda through holistic care and support. Guided by "Rescue, Respond, Rejuvenate," the clinic offers counseling, mentorship, therapy, and digital support to youth, women, and refugees.

LOREMI Tours

Loremi Tours is a community-centered tourism company in Gulu that uses travel to create local jobs and celebrate Acholi culture. By designing immersive experiences - homestays, cultural activities, safaris, and community events - Loremi ensures tourism benefits women, artisans, guides, and hosts rather than outside interests. The company builds ethical, locally driven tourism that preserves cultural knowledge, generates reliable income, and connects visitors to real stories and everyday life.

Bolcoms Ventures

Bolcoms Ventures is strengthening Northern Uganda's digital and creative landscape by helping local businesses, nonprofits, and community groups be seen, trusted, and supported. Bolcoms provides design, printing, websites, media production, and ICT support. They also train youth in web design and digital skills, creating income pathways and professional opportunities.

BackUp Uganda

Backup Uganda is a non-profit dedicated to ensuring every child in Uganda, including those with learning difficulties and disabilities, receives the support they need to succeed. By training teachers, equipping families, and advocating for inclusive practices, Backup Uganda helps schools adapt teaching to meet diverse learning needs.

HASHTAG Gulu

"Street kids" (or more considerately called kids who live and work on the street) are stigmatized by many in the community as violent criminals. But not by Hashtag Gulu. This group of young volunteers values these kids and wants to see them reach their highest potential. At Elephante, they have created a hub for mentorship, life skills, and vocational training with the end goal being to integrate them into society.

Kijani Forestry

Kijani Forestry Limited is a community-driven forestry company in Northern Uganda restoring landscapes and strengthening livelihoods through sustainable tree-planting and agroforestry systems. By placing smallholder farmers at the center, Kijani supports long-term land restoration and climate resilience while helping families diversify income.

NUFA

Northern Uganda Filmmakers Association (NUFA) unites filmmakers across the region, giving creatives a collective voice and the tools to tell authentic stories from their own communities. Through hands-on trainings, workshops, and professional guilds, NUFA equips members with practical skills in camera, editing, acting, and production, strengthening the local film ecosystem. By promoting regional talent through screenings and community engagement, NUFA inspires youth and builds opportunities for sustainable film careers.

VISIT WEBSITE →

Northern Oasis Spa

Northern Oasis Spa is a wellness organization in Gulu bringing professional relaxation massage directly to people's homes and workplaces. Created to address the growing lack of time and access to self-care, the spa offers convenient, consistent, and personalized services for busy professionals, pregnant women, and athletes. Through door-to-door wellness care, Northern Oasis Spa helps clients recover, reduce stress, and improve overall health while building a trusted, local approach to wellbeing.

KALOKELO Studios

Kalokelo Studios is a community-based creative arts initiative in Gulu using art as a tool for healing, expression, and recovery. Working with children and youth affected by conflict, trauma, and substance abuse, the studio uses drawing, painting, theatre, dance, and storytelling to strengthen emotional resilience and rebuild confidence. Through public exhibitions, murals, and community drama, Kalokelo opens dialogue around mental health and reduces stigma, turning lived experience into positive community influence.

Mighty Youth Ministries

Mighty Youth Ministries is a faith-based movement in Gulu mentoring young people through fellowship, school outreach, and honest conversation. Walking closely with youth facing school dropout, addiction, and teen pregnancy, they are shaping a generation that leads with clarity, integrity, resilience, and a strong sense of purpose.

ACTA Event Designs

ACTA Design & Events is a creative production and design company providing professional event services, including lighting, sound, staging, and media production. By offering hands-on training in technical and creative skills, ACTA equips young people with real-world experience, creating employment opportunities and strengthening the region's creative economy.

MTINDO Designs

Mtindo is a women-led social enterprise in Uganda that turns training into real employment through a hybrid tailoring academy and production studio. Women - especially early school leavers and unemployed youth - learn practical skills, gain hands-on experience, and move into dignified work that earns income and builds independence. Profits from production are reinvested to train more women, creating a self-sustaining cycle of opportunity.

AGAMEGA

While most of the urban population is moving away from farming, Deo, founder of Agamega Agro Enterprise, is innovating it and dare we say, making it trendy again. Deo is pursuing crops others shy away from, like oyster mushrooms, and he is sharing his experience and knowledge with other members of the community so they can also create their own successful farm. Deo isn't afraid to think big and we know farming in Uganda will be carried further with him around.

RIBBO Coffee

Ribbo Coffee is a Northern Uganda coffee enterprise building a stronger, more sustainable coffee economy by working directly with farmers and partners to improve quality, fairness, and production standards. The company offers roasting, green and roasted bean sales, white-label coffee, and immersive coffee experiences that connect people to ethical farming from seed to cup. With a focus on consistency, environmental care, and farmer dignity, Ribbo Coffee is shaping a future where great coffee supports thriving communities and a healthier landscape.

VISIT WEBSITE →

WATWERO Dance Initiative

Watwero Dance Initiative is a creative arts organization in Northern Uganda that uses dance and cultural storytelling to help trauma-affected youth heal, express themselves, and build community. Guided by the philosophy of "Watwero" ("We Can"), the initiative was shaped by Geoffrey Oryema's journey of survival and healing through dance. By training and employing local creatives and running community dance programs, Watwero turns performance into healing, dialogue, and opportunity - proving that culture and creativity can rebuild lives after conflict.

Dan's Meat Ltd

Dan's Meat Ltd is a fresh meat store in Gulu providing safe, high-quality poultry, beef, and pork to families and businesses across Northern Uganda. In a region where poor meat quality can lead to illness, Dan's Meat ensures strict hygiene and transparency, building trust through consistent products and customer education. By serving restaurants, cafés, and households with reliable, affordable meat, the company strengthens community health and food safety while expanding access to quality protein.

Scratch Upcycling

Scratch Upcycling is a creative initiative in Gulu turning glass bottles into beautiful, functional products - proving that waste can be transformed into value. By collecting discarded glass from homes and streets, the team makes cups, candle holders, and other everyday items, creating income opportunities while reducing waste. Built on the belief that solutions can be simple and community-led, Scratch Upcycling is building a future where glass is consistently reused, valued, and part of a circular, sustainable economy.

TAKATAKA

Did you know that 91% of the world's plastic is NOT recycled? Takataka is very aware of this issue and they are doing something about it! They are developing technology that is safely processing plastic waste in areas where waste is currently being burned or littered. You can buy recycled products like wall tiles or chairs from this! And to top it off, they are able to employ marginalized people groups, creating opportunities for healing for trauma survivors.

LUMI Solar

Lumi Solar is a renewable energy company delivering reliable, high-quality, and affordable solar solutions across Uganda. Specializing in system design, installation, maintenance, power audits, and backup power, Lumi Solar serves NGOs, institutions, and communities. By creating local jobs, supporting long-term partners, and replacing polluting energy sources, the company strengthens both the economy and the environment while ensuring communities have consistent, sustainable access to clean energy.

Elevate Water

With the sole mission of getting affordable, clean drinking water into the homes of everyone in Gulu, Elevate Water is a self-sustaining business selling clean drinking water in reusable bottles. You can order your water from Elephante anytime! The Elevate Team is also spearheading a project building protected spring wells in the vulnerable areas of Gulu City, providing clean drinking water to thousands of people.
