Building the future of Northern Uganda

Why Elephante Commons

We are an innovation incubator and community center hybrid, working alongside local changemakers in Northern Uganda.

Innovation Hub

We nurture creativity, support risk-taking, and build solutions that are sustainable, scalable, and deeply rooted in community wisdom.

Community Center

A communal culture requires a communal solution. We are building a family of changemakers committed to the greatest good of Northern Uganda.

Local Leadership

True development thrives when the local community takes the lead. We stand behind local leaders who are stepping up for their community.

Our Innovation Areas

All partners must be innovating in at least one of these areas. Most integrate solutions to three or more in a single project!
